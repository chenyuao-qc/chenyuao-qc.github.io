When I worked at Insititude of Quantum Computing, Baidu Inc., I was
- the second person in charge and core developer of cloud-native quantum computing platform [Quantum Leaf(量易伏)](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E9%87%8F%E6%98%93%E4%BC%8F) and [QCompute SDK](https://github.com/baidu/QCompute) which is an open-source quantum programming framework and software development kit for quantum circuit design, compilation, simulation, and execution on quantum hardware;
- the person in charge and main executor of [qcompute-qsvt](https://github.com/baidu/QCompute/tree/master/Extensions/QuantumSingularValueTransformation), a software module implementing Quantum Singular Value Transformation (QSVT) techniques for quantum algorithm design and polynomial transformation;
- a core author of [qulearn(量易简)](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E9%87%8F%E6%98%93%E7%AE%80), an educational quantum computing framework and tutorial-oriented platform designed for quantum programming learning and teaching, whose backup can be found [here](https://qulearn.gitbook.io/) now;
- a core member of the final of [Astar2020(百度之星2020)](https://astar.baidu.com/#/review), a nationwide artificial intelligence and programming competition organized by Baidu for algorithmic and engineering innovation.
